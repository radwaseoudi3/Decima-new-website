# Decima dark/light theme switch

- The existing design remains the default dark setting.
- The floating sun/moon control appears on every page.
- The visitor selection is saved in `localStorage` under `decima-theme`.
- No duplicate HTML pages are used. The switch changes CSS only.
- The approved website accent remains `#8c1b1c`; burgundy backgrounds use white content to avoid red-on-red.
- The world-map section intentionally retains the original map artwork and its original deep-red palette in both themes, as requested.
- Light mode now covers the homepage hero, About, Services, Latest Works, Insights, forms, cards, and ordinary content surfaces.
- Image-led banners, project-detail image heroes, the original map, branded statistic bands, and the footer retain dark or burgundy treatments where white text is required for image contrast.
- Main files: `assets/css/theme-switch.css` and `assets/js/theme-switch.js`.
