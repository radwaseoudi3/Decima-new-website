Our Story

Global expertise. Personal partnership.

Decima International provides integrated project management and controls services to clients in the United States, the Middle East, and project locations worldwide.

Our team brings more than 100 years of combined industry experience and a combination of knowledge, skills, and abilities that goes beyond a traditional project-management offering. Decima has made key contributions to programs and projects across NAM, LATAM, APAC, and EMEA, ranging in value from $5M to $12B.

We remain committed to innovative, efficient, and high-quality project delivery—combining deep expertise, practical insight, and a forward-looking approach that helps clients achieve their goals.

Our Mission & Vision

Built around partnership

Our mission is to become the premier professional services provider across every industry sector in which we are engaged.

Collaboration is the cornerstone of successful delivery.

We approach each engagement as a true partnership built on trust, transparency, and shared objectives. By fostering collaboration among clients, stakeholders, and delivery teams, we help maintain alignment at every stage.

Every project carries unique complexities and ambitions. Our tailored, hands-on approach combines consistent communication, proactive problem-solving, and a commitment to excellence so teams can drive performance, efficiency, and lasting impact.
