Decima’s Expert Leaders

Leadership with global delivery experience

The leadership team combines strategic planning, architecture, construction, project management, operations, business development, procurement, and project-controls expertise.

CEO

Alaaeldin El Hadary

Mr. El Hadary brings over 20 years of industry experience, leadership, and exceptional strategic-planning ability to Decima’s clients. He is a strong believer in the importance of talent, solid fundamentals, collaboration, building partnerships, and social responsibility.

He built his expertise through work with real estate developers, general contractors, and multinational professional and project-management service providers, successfully managing programs and projects on multiple continents. He earned a Master of Construction Management degree from the University of Southern California in 2007 and is active in professional organizations including PMI, AACE International, and EES.

Across his career, Mr. El Hadary has contributed in program management, program controls, procurement strategy, resource planning, preconstruction planning, cost and schedule management, earned value management, organizational asset development, process optimization, systems architecture, and claims management and resolution.

COO

Amro E. Elhadary

Mr. Elhadary has more than 25 years of experience and is a highly accomplished professional with proven leadership attributes, strong communication skills, and extensive experience in architecture, construction, and project management. He is adept at developing business partnerships, diversifying revenue streams, and managing clients.

His experience spans architectural design, consulting, presentations, code compliance, production, permitting, contracts, contractor selection, and the execution of complete construction plans to meet project targets. He has provided creative and process leadership to multiple design teams throughout every project phase and across a variety of project types.

Mr. Elhadary brings a comprehensive understanding of business priorities and a results-driven approach to leading professional teams, delivering successful projects, driving growth, maximizing net income, and achieving strong customer satisfaction.

Director of Operations

Ahmed Tawfik

Mr. Tawfik has more than 20 years of diverse experience across engineering design, project management, business development, and procurement. He has also served as a board member for a manufacturing facility, consulting on budgets, procurement strategy, operational efficiency, and business development.

He earned a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering followed by a Master’s degree in Business Administration focused on operations. His background adds structure to Decima’s operations, with a strong emphasis on organizational design, growth, high delivery standards, and the company’s core values.

Having held managerial roles in several multinational organizations, Mr. Tawfik is a firm believer that teamwork, ownership, knowledge transfer, and client satisfaction are central to success. His focus is to integrate Decima’s purpose, mission, and strategy into daily operations.
